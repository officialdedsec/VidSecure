# Security Policy

The Dedsec Cyber Security Research Institute welcomes responsible disclosure.

## Reporting a vulnerability
If you find a security issue in VidSecure (a way to bypass protection, a crash, a
data-exposure bug, etc.), please report it privately:

- 🌐 Website: https://dedseec.com
- Please **do not** open a public issue for security bugs.

Include steps to reproduce, the affected version, and your environment. We aim to
acknowledge reports promptly and will credit reporters who wish to be named.

## Scope & expectations
VidSecure is client-side DRM. By design it cannot make content "unrippable" —
screen-capture on rooted/jailbroken devices and physical camera recording are outside
what any client-side system can fully prevent. Reports about those inherent limits are
appreciated as feedback but are not considered vulnerabilities.
