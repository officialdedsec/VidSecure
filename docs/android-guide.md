# VidSecure Player — Android Guide

## Install
1. Download `VidSecure-Player.apk` from the [Releases page](../../../releases/latest).
2. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/file manager, then open the APK.
3. Runs on **Android 8.0+**, real devices only.

## Play a protected video
1. Open the app and tap the **▶ play button**.
2. Pick the `.vdx` file you received.
3. Unlock it:
   - **Per-PC file:** the app shows your **Hardware ID** — send it to the seller, get
     a key back, and paste it in.
   - **Password file:** just enter the password.
4. The video plays with in-memory decryption and a moving watermark.

## Controls (VLC-style)
- **Single tap** — show/hide controls.
- **Double-tap left / right** — seek −10s / +10s; **double-tap centre** — play/pause.
- **Swipe up/down** — brightness (left half) / volume (right half).
- Bottom bar: stop · −10s · play/pause · +10s · mute · aspect-ratio, with a seek bar.

## Licenses
Tap **Manage licenses** to view or remove saved keys/passwords. Licenses are stored
encrypted on the device.

## Notes
- Screenshots and screen recording are blocked; playback stops if you mirror/cast.
- Developer Options must be **off** to play. Rooted/emulator devices are blocked.
